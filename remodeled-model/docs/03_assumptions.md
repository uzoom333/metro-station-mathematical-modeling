# Assumptions

- One well-mixed hypothetical station air volume.
- Ideal-gas air with constant $c_p$, $c_v$, molar mass, and gas constant.
- One effective structural thermal mass.
- Balanced nominal mechanical and train-piston volume exchange.
- Piecewise-linear occupancy with a four-person baseline minimum.
- Direct train heat during dwell and exponential residual heat afterward.
- Piston windows extend 30 seconds before and after arrival and departure.
- Outdoor temperature, pressure, and CO₂ are constant within each run.
- Fan energy is a cubic proxy, not a selected fan specification.

Dimensions, schedules, and parameters are synthetic illustrative choices.
