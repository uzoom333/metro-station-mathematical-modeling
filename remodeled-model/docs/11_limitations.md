# Limitations

The original station problem, major variables, logical reasoning, and modeling
methodology come from the award-winning 2024 project. All V2 equations,
architecture, values, simulations, graphs, scenarios, sensitivity analysis,
optimization, and conclusions were newly developed with synthetic data.

This educational lumped model has no measured calibration, spatial CFD,
humidity, contaminants beyond CO₂, smoke physics, fire model, detailed tunnel
network, equipment specification, or regulatory assessment. CO₂ alone is not
a complete indoor-air-quality measure. Thresholds are illustrative experiment
targets.

Do not use V2 to size or assess a real ventilation, HVAC, railway,
smoke-control, or fire-safety system.
